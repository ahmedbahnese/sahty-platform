## Severity interpretation

- **CRITICAL:** healthcare data exposure, privileged access, schema/deployment failure, or a blocker to safe production operation.
- **HIGH:** major workflow failure, authentication/session risk, or missing production dependency.
- **MEDIUM:** important correctness/coverage issue with a workaround or limited blast radius.
- **LOW:** usability, maintainability, or non-core behavior.
